=== criteria ===
Score this document on two criteria.

## Criterion C5: Tone against the style guide (c5_tone, score 1–5)
Compare the voice of the whole document with the style guide and the audience in the brief. In bad-news situations, spin and hype count against the document. Quote up to three passages that support your score; list each tone problem under "issues".
5 — The voice matches the style guide and the audience throughout; no hype, filler, hedging or marketing language; bad news is stated plainly.
4 — Matches the style guide; at most one phrase that is slightly off (a cliché, one unnecessary intensifier).
3 — Mostly appropriate, but with a noticeable pattern of filler, stock phrases or generic corporate language, OR a tone that shifts between sections.
2 — Clearly against the style guide in places: hype or spin ("record quarter", "strong momentum") where the data does not support it, addressing the reader as a chatbot would, or a register wrong for the audience.
1 — The tone defeats the purpose: promotional where the brief asks for plain reporting, or bad news hidden behind spin.

## Criterion C6: Instruction adherence (c6_instructions, score 1–5)
Check each section against its instruction in the outline: the content it must cover, the items it must include, the semantic requirements ("tie each recommendation to a risk", "built around the three themes"). Word and list-count limits are measured elsewhere; judge them only when they are clearly ignored. Quote up to three passages that support your score; list each missed or violated instruction under "issues".
5 — Every section does what its instruction asks, fully, and nothing the instructions forbid.
4 — Every section follows its instruction; at most one small omission (one requested item thin or implicit).
3 — One requested item missing or one semantic requirement only partly met, OR two small omissions.
2 — One section ignores a central part of its instruction, or several requested items are missing across the document.
1 — The document does not do what the outline asks: several sections off-brief, or a forbidden element used repeatedly.
