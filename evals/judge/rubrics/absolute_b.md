=== criteria ===
Score this document on two criteria.

## Criterion C3: No repetition (c3_repetition, score 1–5)
Look for the same point, sentence, figure or recommendation made in more than one section, and for prose that only restates a data table. Quote up to three passages that support your score; list each repeated point under "issues".
5 — Every section adds something new; a figure or point recurs only where a later section builds on it explicitly ("the margin decline noted above...").
4 — At most one point or figure restated without adding anything.
3 — Two or three points or figures restated across sections, or one section that mostly re-tells another; noticeable but the document still moves forward.
2 — Repetition is a pattern: several sections open with the same recap, or the same sentence or recommendation appears nearly verbatim in two places.
1 — Large parts of the document repeat each other; a reader would skip whole sections.

## Criterion C4: Formatting (c4_formatting, score 1–5)
Check headings, lists, paragraphs, tables and Markdown hygiene against the outline and its constraints (bulleted lists where asked, paragraph counts, one title). Quote up to three passages that support your score; list each formatting problem under "issues".
5 — Clean, consistent Markdown: one title, one heading per outline section in outline order, no extra or skipped heading levels, lists and paragraphs exactly where the instructions ask, no stray artefacts (preambles, sign-offs, code fences, broken tables).
4 — One minor inconsistency (an extra sub-heading, a list where prose was asked, a slightly uneven list style).
3 — Two or three minor problems, or one noticeable one (a section heading repeated inside its own section, a paragraph count clearly off, bold used as headings).
2 — A structural problem a reader notices at once: a missing or out-of-order section, a second title, a skipped heading level, conversational chatter ("Sure! Here is...") left in the text, or a broken table.
1 — Several structural problems; the document would need reformatting before it could be sent.
